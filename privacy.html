Privacy Policy

Ochrecut · last updated 27 October 2026

Ochrecut is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the levels you have finished, your best stars, and how many Undo, Extra Picks and Hint tools you have.
  • Your settings: none (the game has no settings that store data).
  • Whether you have bought All Mills, and the tool packs you have received (so a purchase is delivered only once).

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The first chapter, The Clay Pit (30 levels), is free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
