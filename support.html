How to play

Tap a clay cell to dig it; each tap costs one pick. Pigment falls like sand and slides off ledges. Fill every pot with its own colour before your picks run out: a grain of the wrong colour muddies a pot. From chapter 2, two primaries meet in a stone mortar and come out as a mix. Stuck? Undo takes back your last dig, Extra Picks adds three picks, and Hint marks a good cell.

Purchases

The first chapter is free. All Mills is a one-time purchase that opens chapters 2 and 3. Tool Pouch and Tool Crate add Undo, Extra Picks and Hint. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
