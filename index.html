Every grain goes somewhere

Plan the pour

Pigment falls like sand and slides off ledges. Open the wrong cell and a red grain lands in the ochre pot: muddied. The puzzle is the order you dig in.

Grind new colours

In The Mill, two primaries meet in a stone mortar and come out as verdigris, saffron or murex for the pots that want a mix.

Your pace

No clock and no lives. Each level gives you a handful of picks and a par for three stars. Undo, Extra Picks and Hint are there when you are stuck.

How to play

  1. Tap a clay cell to dig it. Each tap is one pick.

  2. Watch the pigment pour and settle, grain by grain.

  3. Fill every pot with its own colour before your picks run out.

Questions

What does it cost?
The first chapter, The Clay Pit (30 levels), is free. A one-time All Mills purchase opens The Mill and The Deep Seam: 60 more levels. Small tool packs are optional.
Are there ads? What data do you collect?
No ads and no tracking. There is no account; your progress stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Every level plays without a connection; only purchases talk to the App Store.
Which devices?
iPhone, at launch on the App Store.
